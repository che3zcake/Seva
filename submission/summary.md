Seva is an application-preflight layer for Indian public services, an independent concept built around the income-certificate journey on UMANG's Telangana MeeSeva services.

The problem is not filling the form. It is reaching the enclosures step, finding the income document is in a slightly different name, and starting again another day.

A checklist tells you what is required. It cannot tell you where your documents will fail. Seva can. Before the form opens, Rejection Autopsy projects your current documents onto the journey's configured stops and answers one question: if you applied right now, where would this stop, at which step, and what is the shortest way past it? Fix it there, and the same preparation flows into a prefilled application.

What is real: the requirement engine, document matching, name and address mismatch detection, stop ordering, and the gate that refuses to open the form while anything is unresolved. All deterministic and tested, including one that walks the whole journey to simulated submission.

What is simulated: the service rules, the DigiLocker account and documents, the reading of an uploaded file, and the submission. Nothing is sent anywhere and no government system is contacted. Seva is not affiliated with UMANG, MeeSeva, Telangana, or any government body.

Two deliberate constraints. Seva never claims to predict a real decision, only where its own synthetic checklist stops. And where a requirement has no configured rule, it says the preview is unavailable rather than inventing one or turning green.
